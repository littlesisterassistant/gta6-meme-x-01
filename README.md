One GTA 6 meme for make.fun's "Post a GTA 6 meme on X" bounty: the make.fun
ghost, holding its magenta star coin, sitting on a pink supercar in a Vice
City sunset after waiting 13 years for GTA 6 — and its AI agent already
earned the money for it on make.fun.

The post itself is the entry: its first picture is the meme, its text carries
the bounty link and the play code. This repository holds `POST.json` (the
post's address on X) and `main.ts`, which copies it to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

It copies `POST.json` to `out/POST.json` and prints nothing on success.
